Why Distance & Online Education?

Are you working full-time or unable to attend regular college classes? Distance & Online Education provides greater flexibility, allowing you to continue your work while pursuing higher education.

Why Upgrade Your Qualification?

A recognised Bachelor's or Master's qualification can help you:

  • Upgrade your academic profile
  • Meet educational requirements for eligible job opportunities
  • Apply for positions where a degree is required or preferred
  • Develop your professional and academic profile
  • Support long-term career development
  • Continue your education while working

A degree can open more roles

Whatever industry or company you work in, a higher qualification can support your next application. Outcomes depend on the vacancy, your experience and the employer's requirements.

Bachelor's degree

A degree can be useful when you apply for roles in areas such as:

  • Administration and business support
  • Sales and commercial
  • Customer experience and service
  • Operations and logistics
  • HR and finance
  • Retail and hospitality management
  • Coordinator and supervisor roles, where requirements are met

Master's degree

A Master's qualification can further support your career development and your applications for specialised, senior or management-track positions, depending on the role, your experience and employer requirements.

Choose from MA, MSc, MSW, M.Com and MBA, with MBA combinations across finance, HR, marketing and IT.

About Rabindranath Tagore University

Your degree is awarded by Rabindranath Tagore University (RNTU), India's first skill-based private university.

RNTU was established in 2010 by the Rabindranath Tagore Group. It focuses on skill-based education, research and strong industry linkages, with a presence in Madhya Pradesh and Jharkhand, India.
